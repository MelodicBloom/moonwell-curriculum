# Module 0 — Ethical Framework
**The Holographic Dose / The Non-Fixing Rule**

> *Before depth, consent. Before change, safety.*

## Purpose

Module 0 does not begin the curriculum — it makes the curriculum safe to begin. It establishes the ethical container that governs all subsequent work: what the practitioner is consenting to, what the system will never do, and what the facilitator (or self-directed practitioner) is responsible for holding.

The two core principles of Module 0:

**The Holographic Dose** — Every module contains the whole. A practitioner who only completes M0 and M1 has still done real work. The system is designed so that each piece is complete in itself, not a prerequisite for value. This prevents the curriculum from becoming coercive: there is no cliff-edge where the practitioner must go further to receive anything.

**The Non-Fixing Rule** — This curriculum does not fix people. People are not broken. Every pattern addressed in this curriculum was, at the time of its formation, a brilliant adaptive response to real conditions. The work is not to eliminate the pattern but to understand its origin, metabolize the original need it served, and reclaim the energy it has been using. *You are not here to be fixed. You are here to be met.*

## The Five Ethical Principles

See `docs/ethical-framework.md` for the full specification.

1. **Informed Consent** — Full transparency about process, effort, and realistic outcomes.
2. **Harm Reduction** — Graduated challenge ceiling, crisis screening, identity threat monitoring.
3. **Cultural Humility** — No assumption of individualist values. Collectivist adaptation built in.
4. **Anti-Deficit Framing** — Shame blocks change. Compassion enables it.
5. **Accessibility** — Pen, paper, human. No technology required. Free access to all materials.

## Module Output

An **Ethical Context Document** — a short written statement from the practitioner acknowledging:
- What they are choosing to explore
- That they have the right to stop at any time
- That the work is in service of their sovereignty, not their compliance
