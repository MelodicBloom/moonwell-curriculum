# Module 6 — AUGMENTATION
**Blueprint & Dialogue**

> *Dynamic, wide lens — practical, empowered.*

## Purpose

Action needs momentum, not more excavation. M6 takes everything built in M1–M5 and converts it into an operational protocol: a specific, personalized architecture for how the practitioner will behave differently in the situations that used to activate the constraint.

## Frameworks

### F7 — The Intervention Ladder
A tiered response protocol, from micro (30-second somatic intervention) to macro (structural life redesign). The practitioner builds their ladder in advance, so that when activation happens, there is already a chosen response waiting — the pattern no longer has to improvise.

Ladder tiers (adapt to the practitioner):
1. **Breath anchor** (3 seconds) — the somatic interrupt
2. **Mantra recall** (from M4) — the identity anchor
3. **Virtue Anchor activation** (from M2) — the ethical commitment
4. **Dialectical reframe** (from M3 Unity Statement) — the cognitive hold
5. **Sovereign Narrative recall** (from M4) — the identity story
6. **Structured pause** (exit the activation context temporarily)
7. **IFS self-dialogue** (from M5 — meet the Exile)
8. **Formal journaling** (full processing)

### The Coherence Protocol (HeartMath)
Heart-brain coherence practice for moments of high activation. Practiced daily for 5 minutes to build the baseline physiological state that makes the Intervention Ladder accessible under pressure.

## Module Output

A **Protocol Card** — a single-page document the practitioner keeps accessible, containing:
- Their Mantra (M4)
- Their Virtue Anchor (M2)
- Their Intervention Ladder (M6)
- Their emergency contact (a trusted person who knows the curriculum)

And a **Coherent Action Plan** — 3 specific behavioral changes for the next 30 days, each tied to a measurable outcome.
