# Module 2 — UNLOCKING
**The Core Constraint**

> *Slow, deliberate, intentional — deep excavation.*

## Purpose

M2 identifies the specific architecture of the constraint: where it came from, what it was protecting, what contract it has been fulfilling, and which Gene Key governs its expression. This is the slowest module in the curriculum. Shadow requires patience, not speed.

## Framework: F2 — Root Tracer / Stoic Dilemma

### The Root Tracer
Jungian Shadow identification + Attachment Theory origin mapping. The practitioner traces the Core Belief from M1 back to its origin conditions:

1. **When did this belief form?** (approximate age, relational context)
2. **What was it protecting against?** (the original threat — abandonment, shame, overwhelm, violence, invisibility)
3. **What contract did it make?** ("If I [do/don't do X], then I will be [safe/loved/seen]")
4. **Who else carries this pattern?** (intergenerational mapping — is this inherited, not original?)

### The Stoic Dilemma
Applied Stoicism — the Dichotomy of Control. The practitioner maps every element of their constraint onto two columns:

| The External (Uncontrollable Reality) | The Internal (Controllable Judgment/Attitude) |
|---|---|
| What others do, think, feel | How I interpret, respond, frame |
| Outcomes I cannot guarantee | The quality of effort I bring |
| The past | The present moment of choice |

**The Virtue Anchor** — the core ethical quality the practitioner commits to expressing *regardless* of what the external column contains. This becomes the behavioral anchor for M6.

## The Gene Keys Mapping

Using the Gene Keys framework, the practitioner identifies which of the 64 Keys most closely mirrors their Core Constraint. Each Key has three expressions:

- **Shadow** — the pattern under pressure (the constraint as it currently presents)
- **Gift** — the same energy expressed with consciousness and choice
- **Siddhi** — the apex expression; the highest potential of that frequency

The Shadow identified here becomes the Core Constraint Thesis and feeds M3's conflict work.

Full 64-key reference: `docs/gene-keys-reference.md`

## Module Output

A **Core Constraint Thesis** — a statement of the Shadow in its specific Gene Key expression, including:
- The Key number and Shadow name
- The origin conditions (from Root Tracer)
- The contract it has been fulfilling
- The Virtue Anchor
