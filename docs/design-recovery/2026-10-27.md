## Repository-specific records

### D34 — Moonwell: an ethical reflective workbook with clearly bounded claims

**Use when:** When recovering Moonwell's curriculum, reflective artifacts, narrative/somatic exercises, or a possible digital companion.

[Recovered intent] Moonwell proposes eight modules including an ethical frame, grounding, inquiry, dialectic, mythic rehearsal, integration, practical action, and synthesis. The core is usable on paper; digital tools are optional.

[Design DNA — proposed] Legible spacious pages, a paced sequence with permission to pause, private reflection, distinct exercises and outputs, and optional ritual/narrative framing. Avoid dashboards that turn introspection into pressured progress tracking.

[Observed boundary] The README mixes clinical modalities, philosophical traditions, symbolic systems, and contested theoretical language. Its “complete” and behavioral-transformation framing does not demonstrate clinical efficacy or validated integration.

[Repair resolution — proposed] Maintain provenance and epistemic boundaries for each exercise. Separate symbolic contemplation and general reflection from clinical treatment claims. Do not infer whose Gene Keys profile is present or combine profiles. Design meaningful skip, pause, private/offline use, and export/delete choices.

[Reimagined horizon] A thoughtful reflective curriculum and carefully bounded companion, subject to appropriate expert/content review before clinical positioning.

[Carry-forward test] A participant can understand the purpose and limits of a module, choose not to answer, keep reflections private, and leave with an editable practical artifact. No efficacy claim follows merely from a polished workflow.
