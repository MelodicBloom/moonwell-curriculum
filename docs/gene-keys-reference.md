# Gene Keys Reference — Shadow / Gift / Siddhi

All 64 Gene Keys with their three expressions. Used in Module 2 (constraint mapping) and Module 7 (Siddhi contemplation).

The Shadow is not wrong — it is the pattern under pressure. The Gift is the same energy expressed with consciousness. The Siddhi is the apex potential of that frequency, approached through sustained Gift expression over time.
