# Moonwell Ethical Framework
**Module 0 Full Specification**

These five principles are structural, not aspirational. They govern what the Moonwell Curriculum will and won't do. They apply to self-directed practitioners, peer facilitators, and any professional using the curriculum with clients.

---

## Principle 1 — Informed Consent

**Mandate:** Full transparency about process, requirements, and realistic outcomes before any work begins.

**Implementation:**
- Clear explanation of the neuroplastic process the curriculum engages
- Honest about effort required: daily practice, sustained over weeks to months
- Realistic about outcomes: significant but not magical; depends on practitioner engagement
- Right to discontinue at any time without judgment or loss of value from work already done

**Rationale:** Autonomy is preserved. The practitioner is a collaborator, not a subject.

---

## Principle 2 — Harm Reduction

**Mandate:** Do not exacerbate existing vulnerabilities.

**Safeguards:**

*Acute Crisis Screening:*
- Criteria: Active suicidality, psychosis, severe trauma activation
- Action: Refer to clinical support before self-directed intervention
- Rationale: These conditions require professional care; self-help is insufficient and potentially harmful

*Overwhelm Prevention:*
- Mechanism: Graduated challenge that never exceeds 6/10 discomfort
- Rationale: Excessive stress triggers regression, not growth; the nervous system cannot rewire under threat

*Identity Threat Monitoring:*
- Watch for: "This doesn't feel like me" / "I'm betraying who I am"
- Response: Slow down. Reframe growth as self-revelation, not self-replacement. Honor the resistance — it is information.

---

## Principle 3 — Cultural Humility

**Mandate:** The curriculum adapts to cultural context. Cultural context does not adapt to the curriculum.

**Implementation:**
- No assumption of individualist values
- Explicit space for collectivist adaptation ("my family" / "my community" as the unit of change, not only "I")
- Language accessible across educational levels
- No financial barriers to core materials

**Rationale:** A framework serves people. People do not serve the framework.

---

## Principle 4 — Anti-Deficit Framing

**Mandate:** Always position patterns as adaptive intelligence, not pathology.

**Prohibited language:**
- "Fix yourself"
- "You're broken"
- "Overcome your deficits"
- "Stop being [lazy / undisciplined / self-sabotaging]"

**Required framing:**
- "Your patterns protected you"
- "Brilliantly adapted to real conditions"
- "Reclaim the energy this pattern has been using"
- "Evolution, not replacement"

**Rationale:** Shame activates the same neural circuitry as threat. Threat forecloses learning. Compassion opens it.

---

## Principle 5 — Accessibility

**Mandate:** No barriers to access based on resources.

**Implementation:**
- Minimum viable system: pen, paper, one human
- All core materials free to access
- Technology enhances but never required
- Verbal and audio versions available for all written content
- No assumed educational level in core exercises

**Rationale:** The people who most need this curriculum are often the least resourced to access it. This is a design constraint, not an afterthought.

---

*The Non-Fixing Rule:* This curriculum does not fix people. You are not here to be corrected. You are here to be met — exactly as you are — and to reclaim the parts of yourself that have been working very hard on your behalf, often without your conscious knowledge or gratitude.
